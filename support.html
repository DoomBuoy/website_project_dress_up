Help with Dressie

Questions, problems or ideas: email ausnagam@gmail.com. In the app, Settings > Help and feedback opens an email with your app version filled in.

How do I turn on the AI features?

Dressie uses Google Gemini with your own free API key. Get a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey (about a minute), then in Dressie open Settings (gear icon, top right) > Your Gemini API key, paste it and tap Test and save. Everything else in Dressie works without a key.

The AI says its free limit is used up

Free Gemini keys have a daily limit set by Google. Wait a while and try again, or check your limits in Google AI Studio. Outfit ideas on Today still work without AI, straight from your tags.

What are the clothes marked "Sample"?

Dressie starts with a small sample closet so you can try it straight away. Remove them with the Remove button at the top of the Closet, or in Settings > Sample clothes (you can add them back there too).

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Settings > Export backup saves your whole closet, photos included, to one file in a place you choose (for example Google Drive or Files). On the new phone, Settings > Import backup, then choose Merge or Replace.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your phone. Settings > Delete everything removes all of it, including your saved Gemini key. Uninstalling the app removes it too. If you sent an AI report and want it deleted, email us.

An AI answer or picture is offensive or wrong

Tap the flag button next to it and choose a reason. The report reaches us without leaving the app.

Why can't I find Dressie in my country?

Google's Gemini API terms allow only paid Gemini for people in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, so Dressie is not offered there yet.
