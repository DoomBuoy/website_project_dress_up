Dressie privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

In short

Dressie has no accounts, no ads and no Dressie servers. Your closet, photos, looks and trips are stored only on your device. Some features send data to Google (AI, with your own key) or Open-Meteo (weather), only when you use them.

What stays on your device

Clothes, their photos and cut-outs, tags, occasions, looks, To Buy items, trips, settings, and your own Gemini API key (kept in the device's secure storage: Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain). Settings > Delete everything removes all of it, including the key. Uninstalling the app also removes your data; on iPhone, iOS may keep the saved key in the Keychain after uninstalling, so remove the key (or use Delete everything) first if you want it gone.

Sample clothes

The sample clothes you see at first are pictures that come with the app. Nothing is downloaded for them, and you can remove them in Settings > Sample clothes.

AI features (Google Gemini, with your own key)

AI features only work after you add your own Gemini API key from Google AI Studio, and Dressie asks for your OK before the first use. When you use one (tagging photos, Add my clothes, Shop a look, outfit ideas, Stylist chat, Create picture), Dressie sends Google, with your key: the photo you chose (made smaller first) and/or a text list of your clothes (names, colours, tags, short descriptions), plus season and weather for outfits. It goes straight from your device to Google; the developer of Dressie never receives it.

Google handles it under the terms you accepted for your key. With a free key, Google may use the content to improve its products and human reviewers may read it. Please avoid photos that show faces or anything personal. See ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms and policies.google.com/privacy.

AI features are only for people aged 18 or over.

Background removal on your device

Cutting clothes out of photos happens on your device: Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iPhone. Your photos are not uploaded for this. On Android, ML Kit may download its model through Google Play services and sends Google basic device and app information (such as device model, OS version, app version, a per-install identifier) and performance numbers, for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google says it does not pass this to third parties. You can turn background removal off in Settings.

Weather (Open-Meteo)

If you set a city, Dressie sends that city's name (when searching) and its coordinates to Open-Meteo.com to get the forecast. Your device's location (GPS) is never used.

Reporting AI answers and pictures

If you report an AI answer or picture (the flag button), Dressie sends the developer the reason you picked, your optional note, the reported text (for a picture: the look's name and the names of its clothes) and the app version, through a Google Form. No photos and nothing else are included. Reports are used only to check and improve the AI features; ask ausnagam@gmail.com to delete a report you sent.

Sharing and backups

Things only leave your device when you choose to: sharing a look to the Gemini app, opening a shop search in your browser, emailing the developer, or exporting a backup file to a place you pick (for example Google Drive). Backup files contain your closet and photos, so keep them private.

Notifications

The monthly backup reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No push service is used.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

Dressie has no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs of its own, and never sells your data.

Children

Dressie is not for children. AI features require you to be 18 or over.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the store listing, with a new date. Questions: ausnagam@gmail.com.
