What it does

Everything you own, ready to wear.

01

Add a whole pile in one photo

Lay clothes out, take a photo, and Dressie finds each piece, cuts it out and tags it: category, colour, sleeves, season, warmth and occasions. You review the list and add them in one tap.

  • Up to ten photos at a time
  • Warns you when a piece is already in your closet
  • Separate Men and Women wardrobes on one phone
02

Outfits from your own clothes

Pick an occasion, or just tap Style me. You get five outfits that follow real styling rules: a layer when it is cold or wet, light pieces when it is hot, colours that sit together, and a short tip for each look.

  • Uses today's weather for your city
  • Skips what is in the laundry
  • Still works offline, straight from your tags
03

Ask the stylist

A wedding, an interview, four days at the beach. Ask in plain words and get outfits and a packing list made only of pieces you own, with a checklist to tick off as you pack.

  • Follow up with "swap the shoes" or "something warmer"
  • Save any outfit as a look
  • Tells you what is worth buying when something is missing

How it works

Three steps to the first outfit.

  1. InstallDownload the app below and open it. Choose the Men or Women wardrobe.
  2. Add a free keyThe AI runs on Google Gemini. Paste your own free key from Google AI Studio in Settings. It takes about a minute.
  3. Photograph and styleAdd your clothes, set your city for the weather, and tap Style me for today.

Privacy

Your wardrobe is yours.

On your phone. Clothes, photos, looks and trips are stored only on your device. There is no account and no Dressie server.

What is sent. When you use an AI feature, the photo or a text list of your clothes goes to Google Gemini with your own key. Nothing else leaves the phone.

Free and open. No ads and no subscriptions. The code is on GitHub.

Installing it

  1. Open the downloaded file on your phone.
  2. Android asks to allow installs from your browser. Tap Settings and allow it.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

Updating works the same way: install the new file over the old one and your closet stays. The app tells you when a new version is here.

Needs Android 7 or newer on a 64-bit phone. There is no iPhone version yet.
